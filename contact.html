Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click the "Register" button in the navigation bar and fill in your details. You can create either a tenant or landlord account.

How does the payment system work?

Our payment system simulates rent payments. When you make a payment, a 1-year countdown starts automatically for your next due date.

Can I edit my agreement?

Once an agreement is submitted, you'll need to contact your landlord to make changes. You can view your current agreement anytime.

What happens when my rent is overdue?

The countdown timer will turn red and indicate how many days you're overdue. Please make your payment as soon as possible.
